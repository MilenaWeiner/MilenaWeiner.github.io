Project 03 / Luminous Treatment Mist

Hydromer — Luminous Treatment Mist

FIG. 03.0
DisciplinePackaging · Product CGI
RoleArt Director
SectorSkincare
Full projectBehance ↗

Brief

Launch a new all-day treatment mist that purifies, hydrates and refreshes skin on demand.

The task was to translate its complex formula into a simple, tactile visual language — mineral, wet, cold and luminous — across packaging, CGI and campaign imagery.

Result

Developed the packaging and overall visual direction, while leading the production and shoot process from concept to final assets — translating the product idea into a cohesive launch campaign.
